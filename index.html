RULES & CONTROLS

  • Goal: survive the night in the ruined city. Fight the infected, stay alive, stay inside the blue zone.
  • Keep walking — the dead only show up while you move (never more than 5 at once).
  • Loot: gather wood & scrap along the roads — follow the glowing beams. Grab armor vests + ammo, run to the air drop.
  • Rex the dog barks when the dead are close. He only nips at zombies — you do the real damage.
  • Weapons: rifle (starts with 60 bullets), steel pipe / club, and one machine gun hidden on the map.
  • Medkit heals you. Stamina drains when you run — rest to refill.
  • Multiplayer: create a room, share the code, everybody presses READY and the match starts together.
  • Phone: left stick = move (push to the edge to run), drag anywhere to look, round buttons on the right = fire / reload / switch / medkit / crouch.
